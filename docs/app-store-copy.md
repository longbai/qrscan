# QRScan 1.0.2 · App Store 文案

[字段规范](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information)

## 简体中文

**副标题**

识别截图中的码，查看链接参数

**搜索关键词**

```text
二维码,条形码,相机,图片,相册,网址,解码,离线,域名,复制
```

**宣传文本**

在「照片」的分享菜单里直接识别截图中的二维码。打开链接前先看域名，也能单独复制原始参数值。

**描述**

用 iPhone 相机扫描二维码和条形码，或从相册选择一张图片。截图里有二维码时，打开分享菜单，选择「用 QRScan 识别」即可查看结果。

扫到网址后，先查看目标域名、路径和查询参数，再决定是否打开。查询参数可在原始值和解码值之间切换，支持复制完整链接或单独复制一个参数值。

同一画面出现多个码时，可从列表选择。照片还可以点缩略图上的编号。暗处可开启手电筒。

识别在设备上完成，无需注册。

**此版本的新功能**

- 可从「照片」分享菜单直接识别截图。
- 相机新增多码结果列表，照片支持点编号选码。
- 查询参数可以切换并复制原始值或解码值。
- 同一个码留在画面中时，关闭结果页后会跳过重复弹窗。相机临时启动失败时，可以在扫描页重试。

## English

**Subtitle**

Scan Photos, Inspect Links

**Search keywords**

```text
qr code,barcode,reader,screenshot,camera,decode,offline,url,query,parameter,domain,copy
```

**Promotional text**

Scan a screenshot from the Photos share menu. Check the destination domain before opening a link, or copy an individual query value in its original form.

**Description**

Scan QR codes and barcodes with your iPhone camera or choose an image from Photos. To read a code in a screenshot, open the share menu and tap Scan with QRScan.

When a code contains a link, inspect its destination domain, path, and query parameters before opening it. Switch between original and decoded parameter values, then copy the full link or just the value you need.

When several codes are visible, choose one from the results list. In a photo, you can also tap a numbered marker. Use the flashlight in low light.

Recognition runs on your device. No account is required.

**What’s New**

- Scan screenshots from the Photos share menu.
- Choose from a list of camera results or tap a numbered code in a photo.
- View and copy query parameters in their original or decoded form.
- Dismiss a result without another pop-up while the same code stays in view. Retry the camera from the scanner if it fails to start.
