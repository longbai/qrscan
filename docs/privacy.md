# QRScan Privacy Policy

QRScan uses your camera to scan QR codes and barcodes when you grant camera access. If you select a photo in the system photo picker, QRScan reads the selected image to find codes. Recognition takes place on your device. QRScan does not upload camera images, selected photos or scan results to the developer.

When you copy a result, QRScan writes the selected content to your device’s clipboard. When you choose Open Link, iOS passes the link to an app that can open it. The destination’s privacy practices apply to your use of that destination.

# QRScan 隐私政策

获得相机权限后，QRScan 使用相机扫描二维码和条码。如果你通过系统照片选择器选图，QRScan 会读取选中的图片并识别其中的码。识别在设备上完成。QRScan 不会将相机画面、所选照片或扫码结果上传给开发者。

点击复制时，QRScan 会把你选择的内容写入设备剪贴板。点击「打开链接」时，iOS 会将链接交给可处理该链接的应用。你访问的目标服务适用其自身的隐私政策。
