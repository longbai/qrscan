# QRScan Support · 扫码支持

QRScan scans QR codes and barcodes with your iPhone camera or from a selected photo. In the camera, a single detected code opens its preview. When several codes are visible, tap the arrow beside the code you want. To open a scanned link, tap **Open Link** in the result.

For help, contact the developer through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/longbai/qrscan/issues). Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Remove private links, access tokens and personal information from examples.

[Privacy Policy](privacy.html)

QRScan 支持用 iPhone 相机扫描二维码和条码，也可以从相册选图识别。相机识别到一个码时会显示预览。画面中有多个码时，点对应的箭头查看。要打开网址，请在结果页点「打开链接」。

如需帮助，请通过 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/longbai/qrscan/issues) 联系开发者。请注明 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本和问题经过，并删除示例中的私人链接、访问令牌及个人信息。
